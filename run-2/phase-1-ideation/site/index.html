This is the factory’s internal case, not a pitch.

Four adversarial deep dives ran against this idea after it won the vote. All four returned corrections, two of them against claims that flattered it. Those corrections are on this page at the same weight as the case for building — a decision record that only records the case for is not a record.

What is verified

Every figure below marked Verified was re-pulled from its primary source on 13 August 2026 by this document’s author — the SAM DBRA index, the eCFR API, govinfo’s Federal Register text, the DIR schema, and the two competitors’ own public price pages.

What is refuted

Three demand claims carried in the original shortlist failed verification and are struck through where they appear. They must never be reprinted downstream, in copy, in a deck, or in the product.

What is unproven

Nobody has been asked to pay for Wage Line. No landing page, no waitlist, no pre-order, no interview. Everything here validates the category. It does not validate this product’s pull, and § 07 says so at length.

01The ideaD1 · D2 · D3 · D9

The rate on the certified payroll is the rate the auditor will look up.

Christensen’s job-to-be-done framing (HBR, 2016) puts the hire precisely: not “buy payroll software” but “get Friday’s certified payroll out the door with rates I can defend.” Defensibility is the whole product. Everything else on the form is arithmetic.

D1 · Buyer

The person who signs it

Open-shop specialty subcontractors, 5–75 field employees, on federally funded construction. The individual is the payroll administrator, office manager or owner-operator who personally signs the statement of compliance. Not GCs, not union shops, not agencies, not payroll bureaus.

D2 · Job

One filing, every Friday

One weekly filing per project per crew: the WH‑347 plus the statement of compliance, and the California eCPR XML where applicable. Defensibility is the specific claim that the rate traces to a named wage-determination number, revision number and publication date.

D3 · Wedge

The rate-of-record, not the form

Free: unlimited single WH‑347 generation and county × craft lookup, no account. The paid line begins the moment a rate becomes an assertion — pinned revision-of-record, per-classification diff since award, classification memory, portal and eCPR export.

The differentiator is a string of characters that prints on the artifact and no competitor prints. This is the specimen:

WH-347 · Payroll No. 14 · Week ending 2026-08-07 Contractor: [account] Project: [project] Rates from wage determination CA20260022 revision 5, published 2026-08-06; corpus snapshot sha256:2ea52e97…, generated 2026-08-13T08:15:51Z. Newer-revision check last completed 2026-08-13T02:00:04Z. Classification "Cement Mason" resolved from this determination's own class list. Fringe credit in 6B is a customer-asserted hourly figure: annualization under 29 CFR 5.25(c) was neither computed nor verified. Wage Line computes and formats. You certify. We do not file, submit or sign on your behalf, and this is not legal advice.
D9 · What v1 explicitly does not do

Union CBA fringe schedules (refused at signup, not approximated) · annualization and unfunded-plan credits · Service Contract Act determinations · any state regime beyond California · running payroll, computing taxes, printing cheques · filing, submitting or e-signing for the customer · holding portal credentials · SF‑1444 conformance · apprenticeship-ratio opinions · any legal conclusion about wage-determination effectiveness — and no human review of any customer output, at any tier, ever.

The correction that changes the business

Eliminating 100% of the DOL-estimated burden pays for the $99 tier only above a loaded clerical rate of roughly $13.83/hour; the $249 tier needs roughly $34.80/hour and total elimination. Time saved cannot carry the ladder above the entry tier. D3’s choice to wedge on rate-of-record rather than on speed or price is therefore not a preference — it is the only defensible basis for anything above $99.

The six judges, including where each one hit us

PMF evidence · 1st · Fitzpatrick, Ellis, Andreessen

A named vendor cashes a named check

“Wage Line wins outright… Andreessen pull is structural: the GC will not release payment without a defensible WH-347.” The hit: the judge’s supporting numbers were the $995–$4,995 setup fees and the $19,500 of clerical labour — both of which the deep dive then refuted. The verdict survives on a different basis: published, self-serve, repeat prices from the exact buyer.

Willingness to pay · 1st · Ramanujam, Hormozi, Poyar

The budget is forced by statute

“It emits the filed artifact, which is what carries Hormozi’s perceived-likelihood term.” The hit, accepted and binding: “CertifiedPayrollPro already sells self-serve at $49–$249/mo with no setup fee, so zero setup fee is the wedge is occupied — the premium must come from the WD revision-history corner, not from price.” That became D3.

Moat & retention · 1st · Helmer, Thiel, Vohra

A weekly filing that gates payment

“The payroll-title → SOC → WD-class crosswalk is the pool’s only asset compounding from customer corrections rather than crawling.” The hit: the judge also called the WD archive a cornered resource. § 06 shows that half is wrong. The crosswalk half stands.

Speed to revenue · 3rd · Ries

Bounded arithmetic, bounded geometry

“WH-347 math and PDF are bounded, but eCPR XSD conformance is untestable without a DIR account and the $49 rate card is free public data reformatted.” Handled by sequencing: the federal form is the launch deliverable nationally; California eCPR ships labelled generated, not acceptance-tested until G2 clears.

Autonomy integrity · 4th · 12-Factor, Karpathy

Most deterministic core, one real break

“Wage Line’s arithmetic is the most deterministic here and its FAR 22.404-6 refusal the pool’s most honest boundary, but failing closed against an undocumented SAM endpoint on a Friday statutory deadline manufactures precisely the angry customer for whom it declares no support path.” Closed by D7 — see § 04.

Distribution · 4th · Weinberg & Mares, Hormozi

The artifact is the channel

“Best free-tool-to-product intent match… but davisbaconrates.com sits in its own evidence list, there’s no marketplace or integration.” The hit landed harder later: PrevailComply already ships a free WH-347 generator with one-click CA DIR XML. The free generator is a defensive move, and must never be described as differentiation.

The override question, answered explicitly

The rules permit overriding the Borda leader only if the autonomy judge shows the zero-human claim breaks on an unhappy path. It named exactly one break, and that break was a property of the pitch, not of the job: the original text said “if SAM is unreachable at generation time the report is blocked.” That is the wrong thing to fail closed on. D7 retargets it: generation always reads the pinned local mirror, never live SAM. A wage determination is pinned to a project at award and does not move; the nightly crawl exists to detect that a new revision was published, not to supply the rate at generation time. Source outage therefore degrades only the freshness claim, never the filing. No override.

Runner-up · Ship Record, 33

The sharpest calendar in the pool and a genuinely elegant insight. Three reasons it does not win: (1) its proven money — FOSSA at ~$207/mo, sbomify at $159/mo — is for the attribution half, not the CRA wedge, which is anticipated compliance and therefore stated interest until the first enforcement action lands. (2) Per-release SBOMs and LICENSE history are largely reconstructable from git tags and registry tarballs by a 2028 entrant. (3) Demand is deadline-shaped: SBOM obligations are not formally enforceable until December 2027, so there is a 15-month urgency trough immediately after the September 2026 spike — precisely the window a new company has to survive. Wage Line’s demand arrives every Friday forever.

04The autonomy gateA1–A6 · hard gate

Zero human minutes, including on the bad Friday.

Run 1’s winner relied on a human in the loop. This run forbids it. A candidate is disqualified unless every point below holds — and the test is not how many fail-closed sentences a pitch contains, but whether autonomy survives the unhappy path.

Right price points. Wrong packaging function.

Ramanujam & Tacke’s test in Monetizing Innovation is whether the budget is already forced and a reference price exists. Both hold. But D4 expressed its value metric as hard capacity caps with 2.5× price steps — and a cap is a wall, which is a churn event, not an expansion event. The price points are kept. The caps are not.

Challenge to D4 — implemented, not silently redesigned

D4’s metric — active projects × workers, both metered from generated filings — names the right driver and the right instrumentation, and both stay metered and reported in-product exactly as D4 requires. Only the pricing function changes: filings are priced, workers are measured. A two-variable metric cannot be forecast by the buyer before purchase, and charging per worker charges a contractor for growing his crew. The market has already converged on the certified filing as the meter — $12, $5, $3, $1 per report — so adopting it costs nothing in buyer education.

CertiWage at $29/mo for unlimited weekly reports — no XML, no revision tracking, but $29. Inside the category “certified payroll software” the default comparison is $29–$145/mo and we lose it. Unit economics — modelled, never measured

Variable cost per certified filing ≈$0.06: ~$0.05 of model spend (classification ranking fires only on a new account/WD/title tuple, ~12 times per project-year against 52 filings; exception narrative on ~15% of filings) plus ~$0.01 of deterministic compute and perpetual artifact retention. 06The corpusD5 · D6 · D7

The archive is not a cornered resource. Say so.

The moat as originally pitched rested on “SAM overwrites, and a superseded revision is gone.” It does not. Under Helmer’s 7 Powers, a resource you can rent for $19 a month is not cornered, and the honest restatement is assembly, latency and crosswalk — not unreconstructability.

What does not survive

Scarcity of the archive. WH-347 generation (free from DOL, free from PrevailComply, $12/report from LCPtracker, $29/mo unlimited from CertiWage). CA eCPR XML — LCPcertified ships California plus Washington and Maryland, so on day one we are behind on this feature, not ahead. Zero setup fee. County × craft SEO, where an affiliate-funded site already ranks. Price — we are the premium option.

What genuinely survives

(a) Revision-level provenance printed on the artifact, with per-classification diff since award — no competitor publishes this on the emitted form. (b) Refusal semantics: DRAFT — NOT CERTIFIABLE, signature withheld, and an explicit declination on FAR 22.404-6, where every competitor either guesses or routes to a human. (c) Per-account classification memory, which compounds from corrections rather than crawling. (d) Counter-positioning: a managed service cannot go pure self-serve without cannibalising the labour it bills for.

The dual-ingest rule earned its place on the first record pulled

Checked today on CA20260022 revision 5: the index reports isStandard: true while the document reports standard: false for the same determination, same revision, same day. This is Hyrum’s Law territory — we depend on observable behaviour, not on a contract. D5’s rule is that disagreement on any pinned field blocks promotion of both paths for that determination and narrows its rate assertions to the last agreed snapshot. Never publish either side.

D5 · Nightly

SAM DBRA index crawled at 02:00 ET with full pagination. Every revision retained permanently, per-classification diffs computed per determination. Per-WD document fetch as an independent second path.

D5 · Weekly

eCFR 29 CFR Parts 1, 3 and 5 diffed Mondays into a versioned obligation changelog. WHD form pages and FAR Part 22.4 hash-diffed — the WH-347 was revised in January 2025 and the corpus has to notice the next one.

D6 · Model boundary

All money arithmetic is plain code under property tests, never a model: gross, fringe credit, cash-in-lieu, CWHSSA overtime at 0.5 × max(BHR, cash rate excluding fringe), deductions, net. PDF geometry and XSD-validated XML are emitted by code.

07What is unprovenread before quoting anything

Ten things this decision does not know.

Blank’s customer-development gates (first principles) mostly pass: the problem exists at scale, buyers actively seek solutions, they already pay, the channel is reachable. The gate that does not pass is the one that matters — nothing has been pulled out of Wage Line, because Wage Line does not exist.

  1. Nobody has been asked to pay for Wage Line.

    No landing page, no waitlist, no pre-order, no interview. Everything in § 02 validates the category and is being absorbed by funded incumbents. It does not validate this product’s differentiated pull. This is the largest gap by far.

    Settled by a live landing page with the $49 bid rate card behind Stripe, and a conversion rate measured rather than assumed.

  2. No primary contractor voice was obtained.

    Reddit, ContractorTalk and the Mike Holt forums were all unreachable from the validation environment (HTTP 402 and 403). The shortlist’s unattributed quote that contractors call incumbent exports “revision hell” is unverified and must not be reused. What replaced it — a vendor’s public UserVoice board — is written mostly by agency and prime approvers, not by the 5–75-person open-shop sub D1 targets.

    Settled by ten Mom-Test conversations with signers of the statement of compliance, about last Friday, not about the idea.

  3. The D1 slice is uncounted.

    122,936 filers is the tightest official count of who has this problem. No source splits it by open-shop versus union, prime versus sub, or headcount. Any “N thousand target customers” figure downstream is a hypothesis wearing a number’s clothes.

    Settled by nothing published; treat as permanently unknown and size bottom-up from actual signups.

  4. Ellis’s 40% test cannot be run and must not be simulated.

    The 40% threshold requires activated users. There are zero. Per Vohra’s method the survey must also be segmented before averaging — open-shop versus mixed, single- versus multi-project — which is the whole point of it.

    Settled by instrumenting the survey from day one against accounts with ≥2 filings.

  5. We are the premium option against published, lower, self-serve prices.

    D4 was designed against a demo-and-quote strawman that does not exist for this buyer. The revised ladder narrows the gap but does not close it, and any tier that reads to the buyer as “a WH-347 generator with a subscription” loses in a browser tab.

    Settled by whether the rate-of-record framing converts at all — the single riskiest commercial assumption in the plan.

  6. On the artifact axis, v1 ships fewer states than a live incumbent.

    LCPcertified already emits California, Washington and Maryland XML. New York’s MPWR portal has required electronic certified payroll for all Article 8 contractors and subcontractors since 31 December 2025. D9 restricts v1 to California.

    Settled by shipping v1 and measuring whether state coverage is actually the purchase driver, or whether provenance is.

  7. California acceptance is unobservable without a customer’s account.

    The eCPR upload runs through the Public Works Online System and needs a current 10-digit contractor registration number plus a DIR Project ID created when the awarding body files a PWC-100. We can generate a schema-valid file offline; we cannot watch it be accepted. The XSD also declares version="1.0" while DIR publishes it as V1.3 — so it is pinned by content hash, never by version attribute. And it demands a nine-digit SSN, which 29 CFR 5.5(a)(3)(ii)(B) forbids on the federal weekly transmittal: same worker, same week, two artifacts with opposite PII rules.

    Settled by G2 — and until it clears, the label generated, not acceptance-tested is enforced in code, not in copy.

  8. Whether the revised WH-347 becomes mandatory on 1 October 2026 is vendor-asserted only.

    Three independent vendors publish the date; none cites a DOL page, and no DOL page was found stating it. The form itself is Rev. January 2025 under OMB 1235-0008, approved 6 January 2025, expiring 31 January 2028.

    Settled by shipping both layouts — revised by default, legacy behind a per-project flag — and hash-diffing the WHD form page weekly. Cost: one template. Cost of guessing wrong: every filing in the transition window.

  9. The fourth acquisition channel is weakly evidenced.

    D8 assumes award feeds identify subs who just won covered work. USASpending shows 52,820 prime construction awards in FY2025 against only 4,186 reported construction subcontracts — sub-tier awards are reported only above the FSRS threshold and are badly under-reported, and grant-funded Related Acts work produces no contractor rows at all.

    Settled by demoting that channel below programmatic SEO in the Bullseye ranking, and testing it last.

  10. Every operational number on this page is a design, not a measurement.

    Median time to artifact, rate correctness, corpus completeness, human minutes per customer, and the staleness credit are all unmeasured. § 08 is the list of things that stay unsayable until they are not.

    Settled by G1 through G6, in that order.

Challenges on the record — implemented as specified, flagged as wrong-risk

D5: the cornered-resource claim is refuted and the moat restated as assembly, latency and crosswalk. D8: the award-feed channel demoted. The legal boundary, stated permanently

We never assert that a filing is accepted, compliant or approved; that a determination is effective for a contract; that a fringe credit is annualized, bona fide or WHD-approved; that a deduction is permissible under 29 CFR 3.5; that a classification is correct; that a cash payment is genuinely in lieu of a fringe rather than part of the straight-time wage — 29 CFR 5.32(c) makes that a question of fact, and it moves the overtime base; or that an apprenticeship ratio is met. We compute and format. The contractor certifies. We do not file, and this is not legal advice.

09Binding decisionsD1–D10

What every downstream agent must respect.

These are binding. A decision found to be wrong is implemented as specified and annotated with a marked Challenge — never silently redesigned. Five carry Challenges; they are listed in § 07.

D1

Buyer. Open-shop specialty subs, 5–75 field employees, federally funded work. The individual signs the statement of compliance.

D2

Job. Friday’s certified payroll, with rates that can be defended to a named determination, revision and publication date.

D3

Wedge. The rate-of-record, not the form and not the price. “Zero setup fee” is retired as positioning — we charge none, but it is never the reason to buy.

D4

Price ladder. $49 one-time / $99 / $249 / $599. No seats, no setup fee, no quote, no call — ever, at any tier. Challenge: caps → filing meter.

D5

Corpus. Nightly index crawl with every revision retained forever, independent per-WD document path, weekly eCFR and form-page diffs, provenance footer on every artifact. Challenge: not a cornered resource.

D6

Engine. Deterministic core, model strictly at the edges: retrieval-and-rank over that determination’s own class list, and templated exception narrative. Both schema-validated, rejected on failure.

D7

Unhappy paths. Generation reads the pinned mirror, never live SAM. Source outage degrades the freshness claim only. Refunds are a self-serve button. No support contact anywhere in the compliance flow.

D8

First channel. The artifact is the channel — every WH-347 travels weekly to a GC carrying its provenance footer. Then the free generator, then programmatic county × craft pages. Challenge: award feeds demoted.

D9

Out of scope. Union CBA fringe, annualization, unfunded plans, SCA, non-California states, running payroll, filing or signing, portal credentials, legal conclusions — and human review of customer output, at any tier.

D10

Gates. G1–G6 above. No public performance claim before its gate clears.
