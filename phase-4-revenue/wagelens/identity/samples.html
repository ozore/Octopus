1 · Colour IDENTITY.md §6

2 · Typography IDENTITY.md §7

Public Sans (USWDS, SIL OFL) for the interface — the register of the artefact without the costume. IBM Plex Mono for every figure and for the rendered WH-347, because a payroll form is a typewriter artefact and its columns must align for free. Base 15px, so a nine-column week fits a 1280px laptop.

Statement of Compliance — deliberately slow

You are certifying three things

  1. That this certified payroll contains the required information, that the basic records are being kept, and that both are correct and complete.
  2. That every laborer and mechanic — including helpers and apprentices — was paid their full weekly wages, with no rebate and no deduction other than those permitted by 29 CFR part 3.
  3. That everyone was paid at least the rate and fringe benefits for the classification of work actually performed, as specified in the wage determination in the contract.

Falsifying any of these certifications may subject you to civil or criminal prosecution under 18 U.S.C. 1001 and 31 U.S.C. 3729 — a fine, imprisonment of not more than five years, or both.
