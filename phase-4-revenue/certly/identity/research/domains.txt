READING OF THE SWEEP
  Every short English compound in this semantic field is taken; the realistic paths to a .com are
  (a) buy a parked one from a marketplace, or (b) take a free `get<name>.com` / `<name>app.com`,
  or (c) take `.io`/`.app` and buy the .com later. Per PLAN.md D3 no custom domain is needed before
  launch (*.vercel.app now, a TheVillage sub-domain later), so the naming decision should be made on
  brand strength and collision risk, not on which .com happens to be free this week.
