1 · Colour

One ink, one cool paper, one interaction blue, four status hues. There is no brand colour: chroma is reserved for state, and the single non-status hue does interaction only — links, focus, and the primary button. Interaction blue sits at hue 215.5°, far from meets (164.2°), expiring and claimed (47.3 / 45.2°) and gap (344.8°). Ground, ink and status are allocated by IDENTITY_ARBITRATION.md (2026-09-03) so that no sibling app shares them.

2 · The seven-state, four-signal status encoding

Seven states, because the comparison engine emits seven — met, gap, asserted_only, not_checked and needs_review at requirement level, plus expiring and no_certificate at vendor level. Four of them must clear 4.5:1 against the same white, which forces their luminances within 1.47:1 of each other in greyscale, and the last three carry no chroma at all. Colour therefore cannot be the carrier. Every status owns a word, a glyph and a fill pattern as well. Claimed, not evidenced shares the Expiring hue on purpose — a ticked ADDL INSD box with no endorsement page attached is a kind of caution, not a new meaning — and is separated by its half-filled disc, its vertical hatch and its word. The greyscale row below is the proof: it is what a gap report looks like in a board packet.
