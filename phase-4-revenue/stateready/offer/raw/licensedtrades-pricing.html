Pricing FAQ

Is there a free tier?
No. LicensedTrades.com is a professional compliance tool — all functionality requires a paid subscription. A 3-day free trial is available for all tiers, no credit card required.
What happens when I exceed my licensed employee limit?
The system displays a warning at 80% of your tier limit. When you reach the hard limit, you'll be prompted to upgrade before adding additional employees. Your existing data is never affected.
Can I upgrade or downgrade at any time?
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
Your data is retained for 90 days after cancellation — fully exportable as CSV or PDF throughout that window. After 90 days, data is eligible for deletion.
Are there extra charges for additional user seats?
Additional seats beyond your tier's included count are $15/month each at Essentials, Professional, and Business tiers. Enterprise includes unlimited seats.
Do you offer annual pricing?
Yes — annual billing is equivalent to 10 months for the price of 12 (approximately 17% discount).
