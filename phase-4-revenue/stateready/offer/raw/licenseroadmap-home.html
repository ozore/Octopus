Manual license tracking is a liability, not a system.

A stop-work order from an unlicensed technician costs $5,000–$25,000 per incident

Lost labor, project delay, and potential contract penalty. One missed expiry date on a Google Sheet can shut down an active job site instantly.

License reinstatement fees run $200–$8,000 per license

A lapsed California contractor license can require full re-examination after five years. A single reinstatement event in most states exceeds a full year of Professional tier subscription cost.

CE hour requirements change — and you find out at renewal time

Texas TDLR and Florida DBPR update CE requirements. The spreadsheet doesn't know. Your technician finds out when the renewal is rejected.

General contractors are requiring documented license compliance for job site access

Pre-qualification RFPs now demand a compliance export. A Google Sheet row is not a compliance report.
