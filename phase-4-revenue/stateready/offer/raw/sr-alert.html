Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about License Alert

What types of professional licenses can you monitor?

+

We monitor all professional licenses across all 50 states including healthcare (doctors, nurses, therapists), real estate, insurance, contractors, CPAs, attorneys, engineers, and more. If it requires renewal and CE, we can track it.

How far in advance do you send renewal reminders?

+

We send strategic reminders at 90, 60, 30, 14, and 7 days before your deadlines. This gives you plenty of time to complete requirements without last-minute stress. You can also customize your reminder schedule.

What happens during the 7-day free trial?

+

You get full access to all features of your chosen plan. We’ll set up your license monitoring, and you’ll start receiving alerts immediately. Your card won’t be charged until after the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime.

Can I change plans later?

+

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at your next billing cycle, and we’ll prorate any difference. No penalties or fees for changing plans.

Do you help with the actual license renewal process?

+

We provide detailed instructions, direct links to renewal portals, and requirement checklists. Professional and Enterprise plans include expert consultations where we can walk you through complex renewals. We also offer renewal filing as an add-on service.

What if I have licenses in multiple states?

+

Perfect! Our system is designed for multi-state license holders. We track different requirements, deadlines, and regulations for each state. The Professional plan covers up to 5 licenses, Enterprise covers 10, and we offer custom plans for more.
