## How to read this table

- **Fit** names which side of the suspension event the partner sits on: **Upstream** = touches the seller before/during a suspension in a way that makes them a natural referrer (they see the notice, or they get the panicked call first); **Downstream** = touches the seller's operations in a way a suspension disrupts, making them a natural bundle/co-marketing partner; **Adjacent-detect** = monitors account health but has no remedy to sell (the dossier's explicitly named "partial substitute" pattern — SellerSonar, Helium 10 Alerts).
- **Suggested shape** follows Hormozi's Core Four (*$100M Leads*) and Aaron Ross's *Predictable Revenue* channel-partner logic: referral fee (pay-per-close, tracked via a partner code), content collab (co-branded guide, webinar, newsletter placement), or bundle (Clausewright offered as a line item inside the partner's existing service/retainer).
- **Source** is the partner's own public page. No field is filled in beyond what that page states; unverifiable fields (e.g., audience size where none is published) are left blank rather than estimated.
- **Contact page** is the partner's own published business contact/inquiry page — never a personal email. Several partner-program pages (BQool, Seller Snap) publish a dedicated partnership channel, and SmartScout publishes a support address; those are used where found.
- **No individual is named anywhere in this file.** Where a prospect's relevance runs through a person — an agency founder, a podcast host, a newsletter author — the row names the **company or the show**, never the human, matching `crm/CRM.md §6.2` and the CSVs built from this file. Contact routes are business forms and published business addresses only.
- **Conflict flag** marks any company found during research that itself sells suspension-appeal or reinstatement services — these are competitors, not partners, and are excluded from the table below (see note at the end).

## Compliance notes for the outreach-drafting phase (binding, not yet executed)

Per the hard ethics rules governing this research: **nothing above has been sent.** Any outreach draft built from this list must, before it is queued for founder review:

1. Identify Clausewright as the sender by legal/trading name, include a physical-address placeholder, and include a working opt-out line (CAN-SPAM, 15 U.S.C. §7704).
2. Avoid a deceptive subject line — no fake urgency, no impersonation of a reply-in-thread.
3. For any row with a partner program listed (BQool, Seller Snap, SmartScout), apply through the program's own stated channel rather than a generic cold email — this is both higher-conversion (Ross's *Predictable Revenue* channel-partner cadence favors the process the partner already runs) and the compliant path.
4. For Segment K, no message goes to any forum, group, or subreddit member — only to the platform/community's own operators, through their own published business channel, and only where a legitimate sponsor/partner channel exists.
5. Every claim of fact in outreach copy about Clausewright (pricing, guarantee, turnaround) must trace to `IDEA_DOSSIER.md` or `BRAND.md` — no invented success rate, per N10/R11 and the brand's "verifiable, not volume" discipline (`BRAND.md §0`).
