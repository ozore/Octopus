**Three consequences, stated plainly:**

1. **There is no "launch day" in this channel.** There is one monthly thread on two subreddits, plus whatever a rules read unlocks later. Anyone expecting a launch moment should expect a reply loop instead.
2. **A launch post is not permitted before standing exists.** `channels.md §2.1` records the Reddit-wide norm — weeks of non-promotional contribution before any promotional signal. A brand-new account posting into a promotion thread is technically compliant and practically ignored. **Post the replies first; post the announcement second.**
3. **The stricter reading always governs** (`CRM.md §3.2`). An unverified rule is a prohibition until a human reads it. "The rules didn't say I couldn't" is how the highest-relevance surface in the landscape gets closed permanently.

## 1. What every launch post must do

**Lead with the free Decoder.** Per Hormozi's *$100M Leads* lead-magnet rule (and `demand-seo.md §5`, `IDEA_DOSSIER §6.2`), the free thing solves a complete, narrow problem — *"what am I actually charged with?"* — while making the next problem obvious. So the post is **an announcement of a free tool that happens to have paid tiers**, not an announcement of a product that happens to have a free tier. That ordering is also the only version a community will tolerate.

**Six non-negotiables:**

| # | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Affiliation disclosed in the first two lines**, unprompted, always. | `community-playbook.md §4`; the binding ethics rule |
| 2 | **The free thing described precisely enough to be checkable** — reason code, verbatim clause with source, critique of what a draft is missing, POA outline, first section. No account, no card. | `IDEA_DOSSIER §6.2`; `demand-seo.md §5.3` |
| 3 | **Prices stated plainly, once, without anchoring** — $149, $399, $49/mo. No "was/now," no discount, no launch offer. | `BRAND.md §4.6`, §2.5 |
| 4 | **The limits stated in the post itself** — what we decline to draft, that we don't publish a success rate, that we never touch Seller Central credentials, that nothing is submitted on anyone's behalf. | `BRAND.md §3.3` levers 2 and 4; N2/N3/N10/N11 |
| 5 | **No success rate, no customer count, no testimonial, no outcome statistic.** | N10/R11, `BRAND.md §4.1` |
| 6 | **No manufactured urgency or scarcity of any kind.** No launch window, no first-N-users, no countdown. | `BRAND.md §2.5` |

**And one thing that is not a rule but decides whether the post works:** per `BRAND.md` P5, the post's subject is the reader, not us. If more sentences start with "we" than with "you" or "it," rewrite it.

## 2. Post A — Reddit monthly Community Promotion Post

**Where:** r/FulfillmentByAmazon and r/AmazonSeller, **inside the monthly promotion thread only.**
**Pre-flight gate — all four must be true before posting:**
1. A human has opened the live subreddit rules/wiki and confirmed the promotion thread exists and its current terms. (`channels.md §2.1–2.2` records that this was not verifiable from research; the recorded rule comes from `partners.md` Segment K.)
2. The account has weeks of non-promotional reply history in the subreddit (`community-playbook.md §5.2` gate 4).
3. The post is in the promotion thread. Not the main feed. Not a comment on someone's suspension thread. Not a crosspost.
4. `channels.csv` shows the channel at `rule_status=verified`.

**Format:** short. A promotion thread is a list; a wall of text in it reads as though the rules were skimmed.

```
Clausewright — free decoder for Amazon/Walmart deactivation notices, plus
paid appeal drafting.

Disclosure: I built this and the paid tiers are mine. Posting here because
this is the thread for it.

Free, no account, no card: paste your deactivation notice and you get the
reason code in plain English, the exact policy clause you were charged
under quoted verbatim with a link to its source, a critique naming what a
Plan of Action would still be missing, and the POA outline plus its first
section.

Paid, if you want it: $149 for the complete submission-ready Plan of
Action; $399 if you want an experienced appeal writer to review it the same
day. Shield monitoring is $49/mo and includes one Rescue appeal a year — 30
days of it come with any appeal, and letting it lapse is one click.

Things worth knowing before you click anything:

- I don't publish a success rate. Nobody in this category can audit those
  numbers, including me, so I'd rather show you the clause and let you
  judge the work.
- Some cases I won't draft at all — counterfeit, IP complaints, fraud
  allegations. You get told that before you're charged, not after.
- I never ask for your Seller Central login and never will, and nothing is
  submitted on your behalf. You paste it into Account Health yourself.
- Not legal advice.

[DECODER URL]

Happy to answer questions in here.
```

**Variant for r/AmazonSeller:** identical, with the notice-type line adjusted if the subreddit's promo-thread format asks for a specific structure. **Do not post the identical text to both subreddits on the same day** — Amazon's own forum guidelines define cross-posting identical text as spam, and the norm carries.

## 3. Post B — Longer introduction post (HELD until rules are verified)

**Where:** a Facebook seller group or independent forum whose rules, once read by a human, permit an introduction or a tool announcement.
**Status: DO NOT POST.** This draft exists so that it is ready the day a rules read unlocks a channel — not so that it can be posted on the assumption that the rules are loose. `channels.md §2.5` records the "self-promotion runs heavier in Facebook groups" observation as a **secondary-source impression, not a rule.**

**Pre-flight gate:** a human has read the group's pinned rules or the forum's guidelines in full; the self-promotion clause permits this post; `channels.csv` is at `rule_status=verified` with a posture that allows it; the account has real contribution history in that group.

```
I built a free decoder for Amazon and Walmart deactivation notices — how it
works, and what it won't do

Disclosure first: this is my product and there are paid tiers. I've been
answering suspension threads in here for a while under the same name;
this is the one post about the thing I built.

The problem I kept running into when answering: most people arrive knowing
they've been deactivated and not knowing what for. The notice uses
enforcement language, the reason code isn't obvious, and almost every guide
online paraphrases Amazon's policy in the writer's own words with no source
you can open and check. So sellers write a Plan of Action against a guess.
A Plan of Action written for the wrong reason code reads as evasive, and
you don't get many attempts.

What the free tool does:
- Names the reason code in plain English.
- Shows the exact policy clause you were charged under, quoted verbatim,
  with a link to the source so you can read it yourself.
- Tells you what a Plan of Action for that code is most often missing.
- Gives you the outline and the first section.
No account, no email, no card.

What the paid tiers are: $149 for the complete submission-ready Plan of
Action; $399 if you want an experienced appeal writer to review it the same
day. Shield monitoring is $49/mo and includes one Rescue appeal a year.

What it will not do, which matters more:
- It will not draft counterfeit, IP or fraud cases. Those get declined
  before you pay, with a plain explanation of why and who to talk to
  instead. A tool shouldn't be writing those.
- It will not give you a success rate, because I can't audit one and
  neither can anyone else advertising one at you right now.
- It will not touch your Seller Central login, ever, and it will not submit
  anything on your behalf. You paste it in yourself.
- It is not legal advice.

Where it's weakest, since you'll find out anyway: for a few enforcement
categories the governing text sits behind the seller login and isn't
publicly quotable. Where that's the case, the tool says so instead of
paraphrasing around it — you'll see a note rather than a clean citation.
I'd rather you see the gap.

[DECODER URL]

I'll keep answering suspension threads in here either way — that part
doesn't need anyone to click anything.
```

**Permitted:** on channels at `reply-only, no link` where the rules allow a mention (`community-playbook.md §5.2` gates).

## 5. Post D — The honest 30-day update

**Where:** the same monthly promotion thread, no sooner than 30 days after Post A, and only if there is something real to report.
**Why this post exists:** `BRAND.md §6.3` sets a review point at ≥40 paying customers where B9 may support a published rate, *with denominator and methodology, in our own register, in the same breath as its limitations*. Until that point, the honest update reports **what was built and what was learned**, never outcomes. This post is how the community sees the same discipline applied over time — which is a slower and better proof than a number.

**Gate:** N10/R11 still bind. If the update contains a rate, a count of reinstatements, or any outcome statistic, **it does not go out.**

```
30-day update on the free notice decoder (no success rate — here's why)
```

## 6. Answering the comments

A launch post's comments are where the position is actually tested. Answers, in the brand's register (`BRAND.md §2.4` R-2/R-3):

**"How is this different from [competitor]?"**
> Use the approved non-disparaging Step 7 sentences verbatim (`BRAND.md §1 Step 7`). Example: *"They're honest that they walk away when a case gets hard. I built the tier that takes those cases instead."* Never criticise a competitor's product, pricing, or claims — including their success rates, however unaudited. Pointing at someone else's numbers to make ours look honest is still a comparative claim we can't substantiate.

**"Does it actually work?"**
> *"I don't publish a rate, so the honest answer is: check the free part. If the clause it shows you isn't the clause your notice cites, that's a real failure and you'll see it before you spend anything."*

**"Isn't this just ChatGPT?"**
> *"The drafting is a language model, yes. The difference is that the policy text on screen comes out of a retrieval step with the source attached — if it can't produce a clause with a source, it doesn't print one. You can test that in about a minute for free."* (True per B4/UA1; do not embellish beyond it.)

**"$149 is a lot for a document."**
> *"It's a lot compared to a template and a fraction of a consultant. If the free decode doesn't show you something you didn't already know, don't buy it — that's the honest test."* Never argue the price down, never offer a discount, never invent a comparison (`BRAND.md §4.6`, D4).

**A moderator says it breaks a rule.**
> Delete or edit immediately, thank them, do not argue in public, and move the channel row back to `rules-checked` in `channels.csv` with the rule re-read (`CRM.md §4.4`). The surface is worth more than the post.

**Someone posts their notice in the comments asking for help.**
> Switch to `community-playbook.md` and answer it properly, for free, in the thread. **Do not DM them.** They initiate, always (`community-playbook.md §5.3`).

## 7. Pre-post checklist

1. **Gate check.** Is this channel `rule_status=verified`, and does its recorded rule permit *this specific post* in *this specific place*? Unverified → do not post.
2. **Standing check.** Does the account have real non-promotional history in this channel?
3. **Thread check.** Is it in the promotion thread, if one is required? Not the main feed.
4. **Uniqueness check.** Is this text different from what was posted to any other channel? Identical text across channels is spam by Amazon's own definition and by Reddit norm.
5. **Disclosure check.** Is the affiliation in the first two lines?
6. **Lead check.** Does the post lead with the free thing? (`BRAND.md §3.2`, Hormozi lead magnet.)
7. **Claim sweep.** No success rate, no reinstatement rate, no customer count, no testimonial, no "trusted by," no outcome statistic. No guarantee of reinstatement.
8. **Urgency sweep.** No launch window, no first-N-users, no countdown, no discount, no "was/now."
9. **Limits check.** Are the refusal categories, the no-credentials rule, the no-submission rule, and "not legal advice" all present?
10. **Vocabulary sweep** (`BRAND.md §4.4`, §4.5, §5.3): *policy clause* not *legal clause*; *copilot* not *agent/autopilot*; *you review and submit* not *we submit for you*; Decoder / Rescue / Rescue + Human / Shield — never *free trial*, *Pro*, *Premium*.
11. **Register check.** Read aloud, slowly, to an imagined person in tears. No emoji, no exclamation marks, no "simply," no "don't panic." Count "you" against "we" — "you" wins.
12. **Guarantee check.** The **10-minute time guarantee** ("in your inbox in 10 minutes or it's free") is **gated OFF** until `ARCHITECTURE.md §9` G6 clears (`BRAND.md §3.4`), and appears in **no** post, reply, pitch or script anywhere in this phase. **No guaranteed turnaround, no refund-if-late, no countdown.** The one permitted timing word is *same-day* attached to the $399 human review, because that is the tier's own scope of service in the `IDEA_DOSSIER §6.2` tier table — **it is a description, not a guarantee, and it carries no refund.** If same-day review cannot actually be staffed on day one, the word comes out of every draft before the first send rather than being honoured retroactively. Never promise a turnaround on Amazon's or Walmart's behalf (`community-playbook.md §7` rule 15) — neither platform commits to one.

## 8. Frameworks applied

- **Alex Hormozi**, *$100M Leads* (2023) — the lead-magnet rule governs the structure of every post: the free Decoder leads, the paid tiers are stated once and not argued for, and the next problem is left to become obvious rather than being sold. *$100M Offers* (2021) — genuine versus manufactured urgency; there is no launch offer here because there is no real deadline, and the real urgency (the seller's daily loss) belongs to them, not to our announcement.
- **Gabriel Weinberg & Justin Mares**, *Traction* (2015) — Community 21/25; the Bullseye discipline of testing a channel cheaply and respecting its constraints rather than forcing a motion it doesn't support. §0's finding — that eight of ten channels permit no launch post at all — is a channel-fit result, not an obstacle to route around.
- **April Dunford**, *Obviously Awesome* (2019) — the category frame stated in full once per post (`BRAND.md §5.2`); Step 7's non-disparaging sentences as the only permitted comparative answer in the comments (§6).
- **Rob Fitzpatrick**, *The Mom Test* (2013) — Post D's "what I got wrong" section exists because a launch update with no failures in it is marketing, not information; and the comment-handling in §6 turns every "does it work?" into an invitation to check rather than a claim to accept.
- **Aaron Ross**, *Predictable Revenue* (2011) — posts are logged and attributed like any other pipeline artifact (`community-playbook.md §9`), so their contribution to A2's Decoder-session count is measurable rather than assumed.
