# StateReady — prospect research

**ICP.** Multi-state or actively expanding trade contractors (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, plus roofing, solar, fire protection, restoration, low-voltage and pest) with 5 to 100+ technicians, who must keep a per-state licence, CE, bond and insurance calendar for every licence holder.
The sharpest fit is the **PE-backed home-services platform buying companies in new states** and the **franchise system whose franchisees cross state lines** — each acquisition or territory award hands the buyer a new state's rulebook overnight.

Collected 2026-09-03. Research only; nothing here has been sent to anyone.

## Gaps

Segments under 30 rows, and why:

- **franchise system (6) and franchise brand (7)** — the franchisor layer is genuinely small (Neighborly, Authority Brands, Hoffmann, HomeServe, Premium Service Brands, Threshold, plus ARS and Service Experts which are recorded as platforms). The real volume is **multi-unit franchisees**, and it was not reached: franchisee companies are named in franchisor press releases and in Franchise Times' Top 400, neither of which was mined this run, and three franchisor sites (HomeServe, Premium Service Brands, Threshold Brands) returned 403.
- **commercial mechanical / electrical platform (13), roofing / exterior platform (21), restoration platform (6), fire protection platform (5)** — these are near-complete censuses, not thin samples. The public trackers name roughly this many platforms per trade; 14 of the 21 roofing rows carry an empty `website` because the platform's own site could not be confirmed from this environment.
- **specialty multi-state contractor (18)** — the category is real but has no register. ENR's Top 600 Specialty Contractors is paywalled, PM Top 100 and Contracting Business Top 40 returned 404, and Electrical Contractor's Top 50 returned 403. The 33 Inc. 5000 rows partly cover it; the rest of the long tail needs a paid list.
- **licence expediting firm (14), exam prep / CE school (10), surety bond / insurance (10), field service software (8)** — these are small, real categories at the national level. Expediters in particular are a long tail of state-local firms; a state-by-state search would multiply this segment but each row would be a one-person shop.
- **national trade association (13)** — complete at the national level. What is missing is the **state chapter layer**, which would be worth ~150 rows on its own: PHCC's chapter finder is behind Cloudflare, IEC's 54 chapters render from JS with no REST endpoint, ABC's chapter directory 404s, MCAA's affiliate list sits inside a Salesforce community, and NECA, AFSA and SEIA all return 403.
- **channel segments (7–20 each)** — conferences, peer networks and publications are genuinely a short list. Facebook groups and subreddits are recorded by **name only**, because both domains are on the fleet blocked list.

## Next steps — the three sources that would add the most

1. **Apex Service Partners' brand list** (`apexservicepartners.com`, HTTP 403 here). ~107 brands across 46 states in one page — the single largest missing block, and the highest-value account in the file. A browser session or a Bing cache would unblock it. The same applies to Service Experts (31 states), Southern Home Services and NearU.
2. **PHCC Chapter Connect** — `POST https://www.phccweb.org/wp-admin/admin-ajax.php` with `action=phcc_chapter_connect&state=<XX>`, looped over 50 states. Cloudflare blocks curl here, but the endpoint and payload are confirmed from `chapter_connect_app.js`. That is the state-chapter layer in one loop. Pair it with ABC's 69 chapters and IEC's 54.
3. **Franchise Times Top 400 / franchisor press releases** for multi-unit franchisee companies. A franchisee running One Hour, Benjamin Franklin and Mister Sparky territories in three states is the sharpest small-account fit in the whole ICP and is currently absent from the file.
