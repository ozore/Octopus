# ScopeIQ — prospect list

**816 organisations**, collected 2026-09-03, all sourced. Research only: nothing here has been sent
to anyone and nothing here authorises sending anything.

## ICP

Organisations that deliver regulated aesthetic medicine (Botox, fillers, laser, GLP-1, IV therapy)
and must answer *who may inject what, under whose supervision, and which ownership structure is legal*
in a specific state — multi-location med spa platforms and MSOs entering a new state, aesthetic
franchisors and their franchisees, single-location med spas, and multi-state GLP-1 / IV / telehealth
aesthetic providers.
Plus the organisations already selling to them (software, medical direction, training, devices,
pharmacies, lenders, consultants, associations) and the rooms where they gather.

## Gaps (segments under 30 rows, and why)

Only one end-customer segment cleared 30 rows (single-location med spas, 554). The rest are
**genuinely small universes**, not under-collected:

- **med spa platform / MSO (23)** — the CT Acquisitions 2026 report states that "fewer than 20 US med
  spa organizations at any given time" meet platform criteria, and puts PE ownership at ~3% of the
  ~10,488 US med spas. 23 named platforms is close to the whole population. More exist as sub-brands
  inside AMP (20+ brands) that publish no independent site.
- **aesthetic franchise system (13)** and **multi-location med spa group (9)** — every published
  2026 franchise roundup (BeautyMatter, 1851, Fransmart, topfranchise, vettedbiz) names the same
  10–15 brands. The named *franchisees* are the missing half: no US aesthetic franchisor publishes
  its multi-unit franchisee operating companies, so franchisee rows could not be created without
  fabricating them. This is the largest addressable gap in the file.
- **IV therapy / wellness chain (17)**, **GLP-1 / weight-loss clinic (15)**,
  **telehealth aesthetic provider (4)** — the multi-state operators are few; the long tail is
  single-location and already lands in the med spa segment (many of the 554 rows list IV therapy or
  semaglutide in `notes`). A dedicated pass over the yellowpages `weight-control-services` category
  would add volume here.
- **online community (3)** — **facebook.com and reddit.com are blocked from this environment**
  (BRIEF §2.7). Med spa owner Facebook groups and r/medspa are named in the ScopeIQ phase-1 idea
  record as the first-revenue channel, but no URL or member count could be confirmed, so no row was
  written for them. This is a blocked-source gap, not an absent one.
- **insurance & risk (1), med spa hiring & HR (1), professional skincare (4),
  med spa supplies (3), compounding pharmacy (7), patient finance (7)** — small partner niches; the
  AmSpa vendor directory (55 affiliates) is effectively the whole vetted population for this market.

## Next steps — the three unlocks worth most

1. **A human-logged-in AmSpa membership.** AmSpa is simultaneously the incumbent (it sells the
   50-state legal answer for $395–845/yr), the best partner and the best channel. Its member
   directory, member lounge and boot-camp attendee lists sit behind a login that this agent
   correctly did not touch. Nothing else in this market would add as many qualified end-customers.
2. **Deeper yellowpages pagination + adjacent categories.** `scripts/yp_harvest.py` capped at 2 pages
   x 91 metros and one category; pages 3–5 exist everywhere, and `weight-control-services` and
   `physicians-surgeons-cosmetic-surgery` are unmined. Raising the per-metro selection cap in
   `scripts/build_csv.py`'s upstream selector would roughly triple the med spa rows at the same
   verified quality, because the verification step (open the homepage, look for injectable keywords)
   is already automated.
3. **FDD / franchise-disclosure and state franchise registries** (CA DFPI, WI, MN, MD franchise
   registration filings) name multi-unit franchisee *operating companies* by legal entity. That is
   the one segment this pass could not build without fabricating, and it is exactly the buyer:
   a franchisee company opening its first unit in a new state.

## Files

- `prospects.csv` — 816 rows, schema per BRIEF §3.1, validated with the BRIEF §3 script.
- `sources.md` — every source tried, in order, with status, yield and the exact command.
- `CLAUDE.md` — steering file: rules applied, what worked, what failed, mistakes, assumptions.
- `scripts/` — `fetch.py`, `fetch_sites.py` (parallel cached curl), `yp_harvest.py` (yellowpages
  parser), `verify_spas.py` (homepage verification + procedure harvest), `loc_stats.py`
  (state counting), `build_csv.py` (regenerates `prospects.csv`).
- `data/` — parsed intermediates (`yp_listings.json`, `yp_verified*.json`, `amspa_vendors.json`,
  `curated.py`) and the URL lists each fetch used.
- `raw/` — **deleted after collection** (382 MB of cached HTML). Re-create it by re-running
  `scripts/yp_harvest.py` then `scripts/fetch_sites.py` on the URL lists in `data/`; `build_csv.py`
  itself needs only `data/` and runs offline.

Rebuild the CSV with:

```
python3 phase-3-acquisition/prospects/scopeiq/scripts/build_csv.py
```
