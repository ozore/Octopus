---
subject: Cancelling your StateReady subscription
when: A customer asks to cancel, asks how, or asks what happens to their data and alerts if they do.
---
Hello,

You can cancel yourself, today, and nothing is hidden behind a call.

1. Settings, Billing, then open the Stripe portal and cancel there. Access continues to the end of the period you have paid for.
2. There is no pro-rata refund on a month. If you cancel within 7 days of your first charge and have not generated a State Entry Pack, we refund the current month in full.
3. Your data stays readable and exportable afterwards. Read-only mode blocks edits and never blocks reads or exports; a compliance record you built should not vanish because you stopped paying.
4. Alerts pause when the subscription ends, and the app says so in words. If you rely on them for a deadline in the next 90 days, take an export first: Settings, Data, Export everything.
5. Deleting the organization is a separate step with a 7-day delay, cancelable throughout; details are in the export and deletion note.

If something in the product caused this, one line about what it was would help more than you might think, and I read every one.
