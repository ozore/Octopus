Hello,

Here is how billing works, so you can check it against what you see in the app.

Plans and prices
- Single State: $149 a month or $1,490 a year. One state, up to 25 technicians. Below roughly ten licensed people we will tell you a spreadsheet works.
- Multi-State: $349 a month or $3,490 a year. Up to 5 states, up to 75 technicians. Your first State Entry Pack is included on the annual plan.
- Platform: $599 a month or $5,990 a year. Up to 15 states, up to 250 technicians. Two State Entry Packs are included on the annual plan.
- Enterprise, over 15 states: no published price. Ask and you get a quote within two business days, or we tell you we cannot help.
- State Entry Pack: $1,500 per state and trade at list. $750 for your first state, and the full $750 credits against an annual plan taken within 90 days. $3,750 for up to three states in one bundle, then $1,000 per additional state.

Annual is ten months' price, so two months free. Nothing is priced per seat.

The trial
14 days, no card, the full product with one state's limits, for the first 100 signups. On day 14 the account becomes read-only: your data stays, exports stay open, and alerts pause. The app says so in words rather than let a paused alert look like a live one.

Cards, invoices and failed payments
Card changes, invoice history and cancellation are in the Stripe portal, from Settings, Billing. If a payment fails you keep full access for 7 days with a notice in the app, then the account goes read-only until the card is updated. A card that expires must not cost anyone a license.

If your question is about a specific charge, reply with the invoice number and I will look at it the same day.
