Hello,

A direct answer, because the question deserves one.

StateReady is a tracking and research tool. It is not legal advice and it is not a licensing service. The information in it is compiled from the state licensing boards' own published pages and from state statutes and rules. Every value shows the web address it came from and the date we last checked it, and every value we could not establish from a public source is shown as not established rather than estimated. A value not re-checked in 180 days stops being shown as verified and shows you the board's page instead.

What we do not do: we do not file applications, renewals or continuing-education records for you; we do not cover county or city licensing, permits or registrations; and we never estimate a fee, a grace period, an hour count or a processing time.

What you still need to do: confirm anything flagged "needs checking" with the board before you rely on it, because rules change between our checks. The licensing board, not StateReady, is the authority on your license.

If you need advice on a specific situation, a licensing attorney or the board itself is the right call, and the board's page for your license is linked beside every value so you can go straight there.

The full statement is at /legal/disclaimer. Our refund terms, including the two guarantees we do make, are at /legal/refunds.
