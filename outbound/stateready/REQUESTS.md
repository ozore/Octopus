# StateReady outbound: requests to the engine owner

**From:** Outbound Copywriter, phase-4 wave 3, 2026-09-04. Nothing under `outbound/engine/` was edited; each item below is something the copy needed and the engine does not do, with the case that showed it.

| # | request | why, with the case | severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Separate drafts, approvals and sends per workbook, or refuse to compose both workbooks for one date.** `compose` writes customers and partners into the same `drafts/<date>/` (manifest and preview overwritten by the later run), `approve` approves the whole folder, and `send` loads every draft in it regardless of `--partners`. | Proved in a scratch copy under `OUTBOUND_ROOT`: after composing both for 2026-09-08, `approve` approved 40 and `send --partners --adapter dryrun` logged 40 rows, 20 of them customer organisations (`log.csv`), although only partner stages advanced. With `gmail_drafts` the customer emails would be queued twice, once per send. Until fixed, `PLAYBOOK.md` §4 forbids composing both on one date. | blocking for any day that runs both |
| 2 | **Block a draft whose body carries the from-name placeholder.** `check_draft` blocks on postal address, unsubscribe URL and from-address placeholders, but `sender.name` falls back to `[SENDER NAME PLACEHOLDER - set OUTBOUND_FROM_NAME]` and renders into the body unblocked. | Every StateReady body ends with `{{sender.name}}` (the only sender variable in the compose context). With `OUTBOUND_FROM_NAME` unset and the other three set, the placeholder line ships. | high |
| 3 | **Reader-facing phrases for segment labels in `personalise.opening_sentence()`.** The fallback opening prints the internal segment string: "I have you down as a platform operating brand in Brighton, MI 48116", "I have you down as a licence expediting / compliance firm". | 260 of 344 customer rows are `platform operating brand` and get that sentence; the partner label carries British spelling into a US-spelling email. `partner-expediter` avoids `fact.opening` for this reason and personalises on `fact.location` only (6 of 10 rows). A small map from segment to phrase ("an operating brand under a home-services platform") would fix all of them. | medium |
| 4 | **Tidy `location` before it reaches a sentence.** Values include ZIP codes and pipe-separated pairs: "Brighton, MI 48116", "Portland, CT \| Essex, Connecticut". | 17 customer rows render an opening with a ZIP or a pipe in it. | medium |
| 5 | **Widen `_states_operated()` and `_brands()` in `personalise.extract_facts()`.** `states_operated` is extracted for 3 rows only; `size_signal_raw` holds state counts and lists for many more ("Own site states customers in nine states", "Partner brands in 22 states listed on its own site", "typically working in 40+ states"). | `godfather-multistate` is built around `fact.states_operated` and it never fires for the 26 rows routed to it; `godfather-platform`'s state line fires for 3 of 308. | medium |
| 6 | **A `state_count` column and an `enterprise_quote` stage.** `OFFER.md` §7 (D4) and `specs/09` say the workbook carries `state_count`, sorts on it, and routes accounts over 15 states to `enterprise_quote`. Neither exists in `workbook.py`. | The playbook lists the twelve over-15-state accounts by name instead; that list will rot. | medium |
| 7 | **An `{{else}}` branch, or per-row subject fallbacks.** The template language has `{{#if}}` only, so a subject cannot say "19 states on your brands page" for the 3 rows with a count and something else for the rest without rendering empty (blocked as `empty_subject`). | OFFER §11's subject line could not be used; every subject is static. | low |
| 8 | **Footer spelling.** `FOOTER_TEXT` says "organisation's own website" under a body written in US spelling per `PERSONA.md` §7. | Every mailbox draft mixes the two. Cosmetic. | low |
| 9 | **`{{fact.trades}}` never renders for StateReady.** `_trades()` reads NAICS codes from `notes`; no StateReady row has one. | Not used in any sequence; noted so nobody adds it and gets a silent blank block. | info |
