In Texas an electrical contractor must employ a master electrician, and that person may be assigned to only one contractor unless they own more than half of the business. The master needs 4 CE hours before each renewal; the contractor license needs none. If the master leaves, the contractor has thirty business days to designate a replacement of record. Multiply that by every franchisee in the state.
