Following up once, and leaving something behind that is useful either way.

An ACORD 25 is evidence, not coverage: the form itself says it confers no
rights and does not amend the policy. The additional-insured status you
actually want lives in an endorsement, and the certificate only hints at it by
form number. A folder full of clean-looking certificates can still leave you
uninsured on a claim.
