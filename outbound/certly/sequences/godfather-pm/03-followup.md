One more, because it has a date on it.

Once a year your carrier audits the policy. Travelers tells insureds to
keep certificates of workers' compensation and general liability
"covering the time the contractors perform work for you", and warns that
"Without valid Certificate of Workers Compensation Insurance we may charge a
premium for work performed by an independent contractor/subcontractor".

A missing or lapsed vendor certificate can be charged to you as premium at
audit. A dated Gap Report on your current file finds that out before the
auditor does.

The offer stands: forward up to 25 certificates, get the report, keep it.
