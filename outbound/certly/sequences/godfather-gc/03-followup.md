One more, because it has a date on it.

Once a year the carrier audits your general liability and workers' comp.
Travelers tells contractors to keep certificates "covering the time the
contractors perform work for you", and warns that "Without valid Certificate
of Workers Compensation Insurance we may charge a premium for work performed
by an independent contractor/subcontractor".

A sub's missing or lapsed certificate can be charged to you as premium at
audit. A dated Gap Report on this year's file finds that out before the
auditor does. Forward up to 25 certificates; the report is free and yours.
