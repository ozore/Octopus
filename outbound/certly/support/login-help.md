Hello,

Certly has no passwords, so there is nothing to reset. Signing in works like
this:

1. Go to the sign-in page and enter the email address your organisation uses
   with Certly.
2. We send a sign-in link to that address. The link works once and lasts 15
   minutes.
3. Open it and press Continue.

If the link did not arrive: check spam and quarantine folders, and confirm
the address is the one on the account (a colleague may have signed up with a
different address; ask them to invite you from Settings, Team). If the link
says it is not valid, it has probably been used or has expired: request a
new one, which is instant.

If your company mail scanner opens links before you do, the sign-in page
shows a Continue button so the scanner cannot use the link up on your
behalf. Press it once.

Still stuck? Reply with the exact wording of what you see and the time you
requested the link, and a person will look at the sign-in log.
