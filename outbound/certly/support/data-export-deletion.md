Hello,

Both are self-serve and both are yours to do, with no email required.

Export: Settings, Data, Export everything. You get one ZIP holding CSVs of
vendors, requirements, certificates, comparison results and the activity
log, plus every original document you uploaded. It works at any time,
including after you cancel and while a deletion is pending.

Deletion: Settings, Data, Delete organisation. You type the organisation
name to confirm. Deletion is scheduled 30 days out, we email a confirmation
with the date, and the account shows a banner until then. You can cancel it
at any point in those 30 days. When the date arrives, documents and records
are hard-deleted; the activity log is the last thing to go. If a
subscription is active, it is cancelled first, then the clock starts.

The Free Gap Report, if you used it before signing up: the files you dropped
were deleted the moment the report was built, and the reading and the
report were deleted after seven days. We never recorded the agent's name,
phone or email from those certificates.

What we keep, why, and who processes it is written down at /legal/privacy
and /legal/subprocessors. Your documents are never used to train anyone's
model.

If you need the export or the deletion done for you, reply from the account
owner's address and we will do it within one business day.
