Thank you for asking. The short version, then where to change things.

Plans, published on the pricing page:

- Starter, $99 a month or $990 a year, 50 tracked vendors
- Standard, $199 a month or $1,990 a year, 150 tracked vendors
- Portfolio, $299 a month or $2,990 a year, 400 tracked vendors
- Vendor Pack, +$39 a month per 50 tracked vendors, stackable
- above roughly 700 tracked vendors: $0.55 per tracked vendor per month,
  invoiced; email us and we set it up, no demo

Annual is ten months for twelve. What a tracked vendor is, in the words
used on your billing page: A tracked vendor is one non-archived vendor in
your account. Certly tracks one current certificate per tracked vendor:
renewals, re-uploads, corrections and endorsement pages never count again,
and archived vendors count zero. A vendor who has not sent anything yet
still occupies a slot: finding those is the point.

The trial is 14 days on the plan you picked, card required, no charge until
the date shown in the app, and we email you three days and one day before
the first charge. Cancel in one click in the Billing Portal at any time.

Invoices, card changes, plan switches and Vendor Pack quantity all live in
Settings, Billing, Manage billing. Changes are prorated.

If your question is about a specific invoice, reply with the invoice number
and a person will answer within one business day.
