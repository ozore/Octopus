---
subject: Cancelling your Certly subscription
when: Someone asks how to cancel, or asks us to cancel for them.
---
Hello,

Sorry to see you go, and thank you for telling us rather than letting it
lapse.

You can cancel yourself in one click: Settings, Billing, Manage billing,
Cancel. No email needed, no call, no retention offer. If you would rather we
do it, reply "please cancel" from the account owner's address and we will
confirm the same business day.

What happens next:

- Your access continues to the end of the period you have paid for.
- After that the account becomes read-only. Every vendor, certificate,
  report and export stays visible and downloadable. Nothing is deleted
  because you stopped paying.
- Reminders to your vendors stop.
- If you are inside your first 30 days, the 30-day money back promise
  applies: reply and say so, and we refund the payment, no questions.

If you would also like your data deleted, say so and we will send the
deletion steps (there is a 30-day delay you can cancel).

One question, optional: what would have had to be different for you to
stay? One line is plenty. We read every answer.
