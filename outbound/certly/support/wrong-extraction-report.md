Hello,

Thank you for flagging it; corrections are the most useful thing a customer
can send us.

What we check when you report a field:

1. The certificate page the value came from. Every field we read shows the
   text it was read from; if that text is not on the page, the field is
   marked for review rather than trusted.
2. The confidence we recorded on the field. Fields below our threshold are
   marked for review and are never used to decide a status.
3. The raw characters as printed, against the value we normalised.

How to fix it now, without waiting for us: open the certificate, choose the
field and press Fix. Your correction is recorded with the old value, the
status recalculates, and nothing is re-read behind your back. We use every
correction to measure and improve the reading.

Two states that are not errors:

- Needs review means we read the document but could not decide this
  requirement from it: a limit printed as Excluded or Statutory, a name or
  certificate holder that does not match your entity, or a date we could
  not read with confidence. A person decides; that is the design.
- Asserted only means the certificate ticks the additional insured or
  waiver box but no endorsement page is attached. The form itself says a
  statement on the certificate does not confer rights in lieu of the
  endorsement, so we report the claim as a claim until the page arrives.

Please reply with the vendor name, the field and what the certificate
actually shows, and a person will confirm what we find within one business
day. Certly reads documents; the values you accept are the ones the account
relies on. It does not verify coverage, and it does not give insurance or
legal advice.
