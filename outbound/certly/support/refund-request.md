Hello,

Thank you for writing. There are two promises, and they work like this.

Inside your first 30 days: money back, no questions asked. Reply to confirm
the account email and we will issue the refund by hand to the card you paid
with. It usually shows on a statement within five to ten business days
depending on your bank. The subscription is cancelled at the same time and
the account stays readable and exportable.

The Lapse Watch, at any time: if a certificate we are tracking expires and
we did not warn you before it expired, that month is free. No form, no
argument: tell us the vendor and the expiry date, and we credit it. On an
annual plan the remedy is a credit of one month of your plan, one twelfth of
what you paid. It is a promise about our warning, not about your vendor's
insurance. It applies to every certificate where you gave us a readable
expiry date. It does not apply to a certificate we flagged for review
because we could not read its dates, to a vendor added after their
certificate had already expired, or to email we sent that your server
rejected; in those cases the warning is in your dashboard, dated.

After 30 days and outside the Lapse Watch we do not refund past months, but
you can cancel in one click and keep read-only access with every export.

Reply with the account email and, for a Lapse Watch claim, the vendor name
and expiry date, and a person will confirm within one business day.
