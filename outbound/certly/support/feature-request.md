Hello,

Thank you; requests like this are how the list gets ordered.

Two honest boundaries first. Certly reads the ACORD 25 certificate of
liability insurance at launch and nothing else, and we say so rather than
let you find out. Integrations with property management or construction
software are export and CSV today, not a live sync; we would rather tell you
that than promise a connector with no date.

To put your request in the right place, reply with:

- the job you were trying to do when you hit the gap (not the feature, the
  job: "hand the auditor a file for last policy year", "stop emailing a
  sub who already sent the page")
- how often it comes up
- what you do instead today

We do not publish a roadmap and we do not quote dates. If the request is
built, the account owner gets an email saying so. If it is declined, you
get an email saying that too, with the reason.
