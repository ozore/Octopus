Hello,

You received a link because one of your customers uses Certly to keep
certificates of insurance current. You do not need an account, a password or
a fee to use it; we never charge vendors or their agents.

What the page shows: the customer's name, the vendor it is for, what has
expired or is required in plain language, and a drop zone. Upload a PDF,
JPEG, PNG or HEIC up to 20 MB and 25 pages, not password-protected. A photo
from a phone is fine. You get a receipt on screen. If the new certificate
still leaves something short, the page says what, and you can upload a
corrected certificate or the endorsement page right there.

Common problems:

- "This link has expired" or "revoked": ask the customer named on the page
  to send a fresh link. We cannot issue one for them.
- The file is refused: check size, page count and that it opens without a
  password.
- You are the agent and the vendor forwarded you the link: that is expected;
  the link can be used more than once.

The most common thing missing is not the certificate but the endorsement
page: if the customer requires additional insured status, the certificate's
tick box is not the proof, the endorsement form is (for general liability
usually CG 20 10 and CG 20 37, or the carrier's equivalent).

To stop receiving requests, use the link at the foot of the email: you can
stop requests from that customer, or stop all requests from Certly. Both are
honoured.

Certly reads documents and compares them to the rules the customer set. It
does not verify coverage.
