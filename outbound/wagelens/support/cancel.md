Hello,

You can cancel yourself, in two clicks: Settings, Billing, then Cancel in the portal. It takes effect immediately, there is no call and no retention offer, and nothing else is required of you. If you would rather I did it, reply with the sign-in address and the words "please cancel" and I will do it the same day and confirm by email.

What happens to your records:

- For 30 days after cancelling, the account stays open read-only. Every payroll, every WH-347 and Statement of Compliance, and every export remain readable and downloadable. Creating or certifying new payrolls is blocked.
- The cancel flow offers the Audit Binder export on the way out: one archive with every WH-347, every Statement of Compliance, the determination as it stood, and a source-and-date manifest with a hash per file. Take it. Certified payrolls carry a three-year federal retention duty (29 CFR 5.5) that is yours, not ours, and it does not end when the account does.
- If you cancelled during the trial, before day 15, nothing has been charged.

If you cancelled because something did not work, one line about what it was would help me, but it is not a condition of anything.
