Hello,

Everything you entered exports at any time, in one click, in formats you can keep, and you do not need me for it.

Export: from the payroll list, Export, or from Settings. Three formats: a payroll register (CSV), the line detail (CSV, with the last four digits of identifiers only, because no column holds more), and the Audit Binder (ZIP): every certified WH-347 and Statement of Compliance as PDF, the determination as it stood, and a manifest with the source, the date and a hash per file. Every row carries the determination number, the modification number and the publication date. Large exports are built in the background and delivered by a link. Export links expire after seven days, so download promptly.

Deletion: Settings, Account, Delete organisation. It asks you to type your organisation's legal name, and it makes you take an export first, because certified payrolls carry a three-year federal retention duty (29 CFR 5.5) that stays with you after the account is gone. Once you confirm, the organisation, its users, workers, projects and payrolls are removed from the product. If you want a written confirmation for your records, reply after deleting and I will send one.

If you cannot sign in to do either, reply from the address on the account and tell me which one you need.
