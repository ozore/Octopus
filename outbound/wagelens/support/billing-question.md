Hello,

Here is how billing works, so you can check your own situation against it.

Prices are on the pricing page and nowhere else. Crew is $79 a month or $790 a year: up to 3 active projects and 15 workers. Shop is $99 a month or $990 a year: unlimited projects, up to 100 workers, the Audit Binder and the weekly link for your prime. Annual is ten months for twelve. There is no setup fee and no per-report fee. The GC Roll-up tier is published as coming and cannot be bought yet.

The trial: your first two Fridays are free. A card is on file from the start, the first charge is made on day 15, and you can cancel in two clicks before then and pay nothing. The exact amount and date of your next charge are shown in Settings, Billing, and a reminder email goes out on day 10 with the same amount and date.

Invoices, the card on file, and switching between Crew and Shop or monthly and annual are all in the billing portal, reached from Settings, Billing. Plan changes are prorated. You do not need me for any of them.

If your question is not answered above, reply with the sign-in address and what you are seeing and I will look at the account.
