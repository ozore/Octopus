Hello,

Straight answer: no. WageLens does not produce state prevailing-wage forms at launch, in any state: no California DIR eCPR, no Washington L&I intents and affidavits, no New York or Illinois state certified payrolls. It covers the federal side only, for all fifty states: the Davis-Bacon determination, the WH-347 and the Statement of Compliance.

Where that still helps: on a job with federal money in it (a direct federal contract, or Related Acts work such as federal-aid highway or federally assisted housing), the WH-347 is due alongside the state form, and that half is what we do. On a purely state-funded job we are not the right tool, and I would rather say so now than have you find out in week two.

If you are inside the free trial, cancel in two clicks from Settings, Billing, before day 15 and nothing is charged. If you want to hear whether state forms ever ship, reply with the words "state forms" and I will keep your address for that one message and nothing else.
