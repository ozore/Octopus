Hello,

Thank you for this. I keep every request with the account it came from, and when something ships I write back to the people who asked for it.

Two honest things about how I handle them. First, I do not promise dates, and something being on our list is not a commitment; I would rather tell you it is not built than have you plan around it. Second, the requests that move fastest describe the job, not the feature: what you are doing on Friday afternoon, where it breaks, and what you do instead today. If you can spare three lines on that, it helps more than a screenshot of another product.

For reference, what WageLens will not build, on purpose: it will not choose a worker's classification, it will not run payroll or move money, and it will not sign or file your Statement of Compliance. Those are yours.
