## clause: amz.restricted#compliance-with-law
heading: Products must comply with law and with Amazon's restrictions
obligation_type: requirement
reason_codes: [AMZ.SAFETY.RESTRICTED, AMZ.SAFETY.GPSR]
status: active
excerpt: "We may restrict sellers from selling certain types of products for various reasons, including regulatory compliance, product safety"

Amazon states that it may restrict sellers from selling certain types of products for a range of reasons, naming regulatory compliance, product safety, and work with brands to achieve the best customer experience. It reserves the right to judge, in its sole discretion, whether a listing is appropriate, and to remove a detail page or listing it considers prohibited, potentially illegal or inappropriate without prior notice.

The document also disclaims its own examples: they are not exhaustive and are not intended as legal advice, and Amazon does not warrant the accuracy of information in any links it provides. A restricted-products appeal that relies on the absence of a product from the published examples is relying on a list the policy says is incomplete.

## clause: amz.restricted#corrective-actions
heading: Corrective actions for restricted-product violations
obligation_type: standard
reason_codes: [AMZ.SAFETY.RESTRICTED]
status: active
excerpt: "including but not limited to the cancellation of the listing, immediately suspending or terminating selling privileges, destroying inventory"

Where a seller lists products that breach the restricted-products policy, the stated corrective actions include cancelling the listing, immediately suspending or terminating selling privileges, destroying inventory in fulfilment centres without reimbursement, returning inventory, and terminating the business relationship.

Inventory destruction without reimbursement is the part sellers most often do not know is on the list, and it is the reason a restricted-products issue is time-critical in a way an ordinary metrics issue is not.

## clause: amz.restricted#seller-legal-liability
heading: Sellers are legally liable and must know the parameters
obligation_type: requirement
reason_codes: [AMZ.SAFETY.PRODUCT]
status: active
excerpt: "sellers are legally liable for their actions and transactions, sellers must know the legal parameters surrounding any product they offer"

The policy places legal liability for actions and transactions on the seller and requires the seller to know the legal parameters surrounding any product they offer for sale — expressly including sellers located outside the marketplace's own country. It notes that selling illegal or unsafe products can lead to legal action, including civil and criminal penalties.

The framing matters for an appeal. Under this clause, "our supplier told us it was compliant" is not a defence to the obligation; it is at best evidence about the root cause. The corrective action Amazon is looking for is the verification step that was missing, not the assurance that was relied on.
