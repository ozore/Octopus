## clause: wmt.coc#act-fairly
heading: Act fairly and with respect
obligation_type: requirement
reason_codes: [WMT.COC.CONDUCT]
status: active
excerpt: "Acting with integrity and respect is core to the Walmart Code of Conduct."

The first principle grounds seller conduct in Walmart's own corporate code: acting with integrity and respect is core to it. The violations named under this heading are misusing customer information, providing misleading product details, unfair pricing, manipulating reviews, and unprofessional conduct.

It is a broad principle with concrete instances, which is what makes it the catch-all clause in a Walmart conduct notice — and the reason an appeal here should name which of the five instances is at issue rather than answering the principle in the abstract.

## clause: wmt.coc#accurate-information
heading: Provide accurate information
obligation_type: requirement
reason_codes: [WMT.COC.CONDUCT]
status: active
excerpt: "You must provide accurate and current information to Walmart in Seller Center and to customers."

Sellers must provide accurate and current information both to Walmart through Seller Center and to customers. The principle carries mechanical requirements alongside the general one: display names between 3 and 30 characters, company descriptions between 20 and 1,000 characters, and no use of Walmart branding or personal information in either.

The character limits are the kind of rule an account trips without any bad intent, and they are checkable in seconds — worth confirming before an appeal argues about anything harder.

## clause: wmt.coc#diverting-customers
heading: Diverting customers or transactions
obligation_type: prohibition
reason_codes: [WMT.COC.CONDUCT]
status: active
excerpt: "Any attempt to direct customers to external websites, platforms or storefronts outside of Walmart Marketplace is prohibited."

Any attempt to direct customers to external websites, platforms or storefronts outside Walmart Marketplace is prohibited, and the prohibition is stated to cover advertisements, URLs and social-media links in communications.

This is the Walmart twin of `amz.coc#circumvent-sales-process`. A seller operating on both marketplaces who fixes only the Amazon-facing artefacts has fixed half the exposure, and an appeal that says so — showing the inventory of every channel, not one platform's — is materially stronger.

## clause: wmt.coc#responsible-communication
heading: Practice responsible communication
obligation_type: prohibition
reason_codes: [WMT.COC.CONDUCT]
status: active
excerpt: "Don't send unsolicited communications to customers except that which is necessary for completing an order."

Unsolicited communications to customers are prohibited except where necessary to complete an order, and all customer contact must route through Seller Center in line with the Customer Care Policy.

The exception is defined by necessity to the order, not by the existence of a customer relationship — the same narrow shape Amazon uses at `amz.psaa#inappropriate-email`.

## clause: wmt.coc#meet-performance-standards
heading: Meet Seller Performance Standards
obligation_type: requirement
reason_codes: [WMT.PERF.STANDARDS, WMT.COC.CONDUCT]
status: active
excerpt: null

The code of conduct incorporates the performance standards by reference: sellers must maintain acceptable metrics for cancellation rate, on-time delivery, tracking validity, response time and feedback ratings, monitored through the Performance dashboard.

The incorporation matters for classification. A Walmart notice citing the code of conduct may in substance be a metrics case, and the published numbers live at `wmt.perf`, not here.

## clause: wmt.coc#adhere-retailer-policies
heading: Adhere to all Retailer Policies
obligation_type: requirement
reason_codes: [WMT.AGREEMENT.RETAILER, WMT.COC.CONDUCT]
status: active
excerpt: null

Compliance with all Walmart Marketplace Seller Retailer Policies is mandatory under the code of conduct.

This is the hinge between the conduct code and the agreement: a retailer-policy breach is simultaneously a code-of-conduct breach, which is why these two reason codes are marked confusable with one another.

## clause: wmt.coc#consequences
heading: Consequences of a code of conduct violation
obligation_type: standard
reason_codes: [WMT.COC.CONDUCT]
status: active
excerpt: "Failure to adhere to this or any Walmart policy may result in removal of listings, account suspension or account termination."

Failure to adhere to this or any Walmart policy may result in removal of listings, account suspension, or account termination.

Note the scope: "this or any Walmart policy." The code of conduct's own consequences clause is the enforcement statement for the whole policy set, not only for conduct.
