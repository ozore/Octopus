## clause: wmt.trust#scope
heading: Scope of the Trust and Safety Policy
obligation_type: standard
reason_codes: [WMT.TRUST.SAFETY]
status: active
excerpt: "Walmart's Trust and Safety division may restrict the sale of products at its discretion that don't support our commitments and goals."

Walmart states that its Trust and Safety division may restrict the sale of products at its discretion where they do not support Walmart's commitments and goals.

The discretion is explicit and the standard is Walmart's own commitments rather than an external rule, so an appeal under this policy is not primarily a legal-compliance argument. It is an argument about fit, and it lands better when it accepts the discretion and addresses the concern than when it disputes the authority.

## clause: wmt.trust#offensive-products
heading: Offensive product violations
obligation_type: prohibition
reason_codes: [WMT.TRUST.SAFETY]
status: active
excerpt: "an item that's offensive in nature"

The second category is offensive products: a listing featuring an item that is offensive in nature is removed.

This is the category where automated enforcement most often catches a listing whose text or imagery reads differently out of context, so an appeal here usually needs to supply the context the reviewer did not have.

## clause: wmt.trust#intellectual-property
heading: Intellectual property violations
obligation_type: prohibition
reason_codes: [WMT.TRUST.SAFETY]
status: active
excerpt: "that infringes on intellectual property rights"

The third category is intellectual property: an item that infringes intellectual property rights is taken down.

Where a Walmart notice is in this category, the same caution applies as on the Amazon side — a rights-holder complaint is a dispute with a third party, and the platform is not the decision-maker on the underlying right.

## clause: wmt.trust#enforcement
heading: Enforcement actions
obligation_type: standard
reason_codes: [WMT.TRUST.SAFETY]
status: active
excerpt: "unpublished and may experience restricted sales, account suspension or termination of your selling privileges on Walmart.com"

Products violating these standards are unpublished, and the seller may face restricted sales, account suspension, or termination of selling privileges on Walmart.com.

The escalation runs from the item to the account, which is why an item-level unpublish under this policy should be treated as an account-level warning rather than an isolated listing problem.

## clause: wmt.trust#appeal
heading: Disputing a Trust and Safety removal
obligation_type: requirement
reason_codes: [WMT.TRUST.SAFETY]
status: active
excerpt: "review your appeal and determine if the item can be reinstated"

Sellers may dispute removals through the Seller Support help function, and Walmart states it will review the appeal and determine whether the item can be reinstated.

The stated unit of reinstatement is the item. An appeal drafted as an account-level plan, with no specific item and no specific reason for that item, is asking Walmart a question this route does not answer.
